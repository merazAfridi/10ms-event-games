# কৃতী শিক্ষার্থী সংবর্ধনা – Book of Games

This is a game website for the 10 Minute School "কৃতী শিক্ষার্থী সংবর্ধনা" event. Students scan a QR code and pick a book. The book opens and a short game starts. If they win they get a gift code to show at the gift desk.

## The games

**বাংলা – বাংলা চ্যালেঞ্জ**
Seven questions picked at random from 44, for class 6 to 8: spelling, the odd word out, বাগধারা (one comes as a comic strip), বিপরীত শব্দ, সমার্থক শব্দ, এক কথায় প্রকাশ, and pictures of writers. A game never has more than 2 of the same kind. Each has 4 options and you answer all of them. Get 5 or more right within 1:30 to win.

**English – Verb Hunt!**
One short story, picked at random from five (each has 35 verbs or fewer). Tap every verb you can find and press Check. Green means correct. Red means wrong. Yellow means you missed it. Wrong taps lower your score. Get 85% or more within 1:30 to win.

**Math – Shape Challenge**
Each game picks three shapes at random from five: a circle, a triangle, a square, a pentagon and a hexagon. One appears on the screen. Draw it with your finger. The game checks how close your drawing is. If you get 85% or more the shape is done and the next one comes. Finish 2 shapes in 1:30, with at most 7 tries (drawings), to win.

**Science – Science Challenge**
You get seven picture questions, picked at random from thirty. The answer that looks right is often wrong. A wrong answer shows a funny "Science Fail!" animation. A right answer explains the science. Answer all seven within 1:30; get 5 or more right to win.

On the shelf each book shows its difficulty: বাংলা and Science are Easy, English and Math are Hard.

Questions, stories and shapes are shuffled every game, so watching a friend play doesn't give away the answers.

It works on the smartboard and on phones. Hold the phone upright.

## How the rewards work

When you win you see a "Challenge Completed!" screen with a gift code. If you don't win you see your score and you can play again.

| Game | To win |
|---|---|
| বাংলা | 5 out of 7 correct in 1:30 |
| English | 85% on the story in 1:30 |
| Math | 2 shapes in 1:30, max 7 tries |
| Science | 5 out of 7 correct in 1:30 |

### The gift code

A gift code looks like `1432-7KQX3`.

- `1432` is the time you won (2:32 PM).
- `7KQX3` is a check part. It stops people from making up fake codes.

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Works offline as a PWA.
